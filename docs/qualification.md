# Qualification details

The provider must prove that a named store cannot be redirected by renaming/replacing its configured pathname after opening, a final symlink is refused where applicable, lock acquisition never waits, current selection names a complete immutable generation, writable selectors, multiply-linked selectors, selector symlinks, and writable selected generations are refused, incomplete temporary work is never authoritative, exact republication and stale refusal are truthful, and read-only diagnostics do not initialize or mutate storage.
